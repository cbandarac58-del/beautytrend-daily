2026 Nail Trends: Stylish Manicure Ideas

Nail trends are a great way to explore personal style. From simple, natural manicures to creative nail art, there are many designs to consider.

1. Minimalist Nail Designs

Minimalist manicures use clean lines, subtle colours and simple details. They are a useful choice for everyday wear.

2. Choosing Nail Colours

Choose colours that complement your personal style and the occasion. Neutral shades offer a subtle look, while brighter colours can create a bold statement.

3. Nail Care Tips

- Keep nails clean and dry.
- Moisturise your hands and cuticles.
- Avoid picking or peeling nail products.
- Stop using products that cause irritation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a manicure?

Consider your nail length, daily activities, preferred colours and how much maintenance you want.

How can I maintain healthy-looking nails?

Use gentle nail-care practices, moisturise regularly and avoid harsh removal methods.

Opt for light-reflective powders over oil-heavy formulas for targeted glow. Cream/liquid blushes can work beautifully, but apply them *before* any powder and choose formulas that blend seamlessly without slipping or feeling heavy. The key is to distinguish between intentional dewiness and uncontrolled oiliness.\n*   **Acne-Prone Skin:** For those with acne-prone skin, choose non-comedogenic formulas meticulously to avoid clogging pores. Ingredients like jojoba oil, for instance, are non-comedogenic and can actually help regulate sebum production, making them beneficial even for acne-prone individuals.\n\n**Face Shape Suitability (Blush Placement):**\n\nStrategic blush placement is paramount to enhancing bone structure and achieving a balanced, harmonious look.\n\n*   **Oval Faces:** The most versatile face shape. Avoid placing blush directly on the apples, as this can emphasize roundness.\n*   **Heart-Shaped Faces:** Place blush below the apples of the cheeks, blending downward and outward toward the ears. This technique adds width and softness to the lower half of the face, balancing a wider forehead.\n*   **Square Faces:** To soften angles and add a touch of roundness, sweep blush diagonally across the cheekbones in a gentle upward arc, or concentrate it on the apples of the cheeks. The movement in the hair beautifully complements the subtle play of light on the skin.\n*   **Curly & Coily Hair:** Bouncy curls or voluminous coily styles create a magnificent frame for the face, emphasizing the natural radiance of the skin.
